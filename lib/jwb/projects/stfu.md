## Making a plan

STFU.ai was a web app for arranging events and coffee chats between busy friends. Someone could sign in with a phone number, create an event, and share it with a friend.

## Phone numbers as the connection

Twilio handled both sign-in and messaging around invitations, RSVPs, and coordination. The product brought those steps into one flow, from creating a plan to getting a response.

The backend used Elixir and Phoenix, with a SvelteKit and Tailwind CSS frontend.

## In use

The app saw some usage, mainly in New York City.
